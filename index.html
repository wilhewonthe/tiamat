Critical Finding #1

Energy budget never reconciled

The transcript sized the system at ~5 kWh/day, then at 38.4 kWh/day. Both numbers remain live. Hardware is still sized to the first while the load is the second. The ledger shows a deficit at every duty cycle.

Critical Finding #2

Roof delamination risk

Crane Composites fiberglass on Alfa coaches has documented osmotic blistering. VHB bonds to the skin only — a 20 sq ft panel could depart at highway speed if the skin has separated from the substrate.

Critical Finding #3

LiFePO4 cold charging lockout

Bank in an unheated basement = winter generation uncollectable below 0°C BMS lockout. Self-heating batteries are mandatory, not optional. Winter is a genuine generator season.

Architecture Forks (decide these first)

1. Inverter/Charger

Separate MPPT + Victron Quattro 48/5000 vs EG4 18k all-in-one. Separate MPPT has no solar input ceiling — wins when generation is the constraint.

Recommendation: Separate MPPT + Victron Quattro

2. GPU Node Sizing

4×5070 on EPYC: ~250W idle (kills autonomy). 2×4090 on consumer CPU: ~120W idle (saves 2.4 kWh/day = 1/5 of budget). Size to surplus, not ambition.

Recommendation: 2× RTX 4090, consumer CPU
